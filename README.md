## About these materials
These materials were developed and each tested live with undergraduate and graduate students across Manitoba, and form a subset of a broader set of university and college lecture notes that i am developing for STEM education. They employ interactive engagement methods, scaffolded, collaborative, and independent problem solving, group discussion, intentional hooks for addressing common misconceptions, etc. Instructor versions of the notes are available upon reasonable request.
